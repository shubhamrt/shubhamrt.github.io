How source pathways are identified

Each pathway is traced backward for 24 hours in 3-hour steps. ECMWF 10 m winds represent near-surface transport and 850 hPa winds represent elevated transport. Upwind sections are ranked using surface wind strength, topsoil dryness, MODIS NDVI and support from other active dust hotspots along the corridor. The first 6 hours are treated mainly as transport so the receptor is not automatically labelled as its own source.
