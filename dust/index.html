How source identification works

From dust signal to likely source

1 · BacktraceStarting at the selected dust event, the tracker steps backward every 3 hours for 18 hours using ECMWF 10 m wind speed and direction.
2 · Surface screeningEach upwind point is sampled for shallow soil moisture and MODIS NDVI. Sparse vegetation and dry surface conditions increase source plausibility.
3 · Source scoreThe present screening score weights wind strength 48%, low vegetation 28% and surface dryness 24%. These weights are heuristic and not yet calibrated.
4 · Ecology interpretationNDVI and soil moisture are translated into a simple dryland surface description such as bare desert, semi-desert or sparse steppe. It is an ecological inference, not a formal land-cover classification.
The highlighted source circle is an uncertainty zone, not a mapped emission footprint. Dust transported above the surface can follow winds that differ from 10 m winds, so this first version should be interpreted as source screening rather than definitive attribution.
Dust: CAMS via Open-Meteo. Wind and soil moisture: ECMWF/Open-Meteo. NDVI used internally for source screening: NASA GIBS MODIS Terra rolling 8-day NDVI. Map © OpenStreetMap contributors.
