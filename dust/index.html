Dust fact
Strong winds can lift mineral particles from dry, bare surfaces and move them far beyond the source region.
Source: World Meteorological Organization
How to read this

This is a screening tracker, not an official warning service. It samples a watch network of dust-prone and downwind regions and flags high modelled near-surface dust. A formal sand or dust storm is also defined by observed weather and reduced visibility. Always follow national meteorological and health authorities for warnings.
