The Ground Beneath Her Feet

How Land Degradation Falls Hardest on the People Who Least Caused It

Picture a woman walking. She is walking before sunrise because she walked at this hour yesterday and the day before and every day she can remember. She is walking to collect water. The well that used to be twenty minutes from her house now takes forty, because the vegetation that once held the surrounding soil has thinned, the ground has crusted over, and the springs that fed the shallower wells have dried.
