Mongolia Rangeland Watch

Evidence before grazing.

A research prototype combining current satellite vegetation observations with recent moisture conditions and the rangeland recovery framework used in Mongolia to support more transparent grazing decisions.

Important boundaryThis site does not create legal grazing bans and does not infer carrying capacity from NDVI alone. “Rest” is an ecological recommendation. Legal closures must come from competent Mongolian authorities.

Decision logic

The prototype deliberately separates current vegetation signal from ecological recovery need. A temporarily green but degraded site is therefore not automatically labelled suitable for grazing.

Code noteValidation roadmap for this prototype
  1. Find the newest usable Sentinel-2 scene. Cloud, snow, water and shadow pixels are screened with the Sentinel-2 Scene Classification Layer.
  2. Calculate local NDVI. Red and near-infrared surface reflectance are sampled at the selected point through the Planetary Computer Data API.
  3. Compare like with like. Current NDVI is compared with valid observations from approximately the same time of year in up to five previous years. At least three prior years are required before a relative class is shown.
  4. Add environmental context. Recent precipitation, reference evapotranspiration and modelled near-surface soil moisture are reported separately. They are not labelled as satellite measurements.
  5. Apply the recovery constraint. A supplied recovery class can override temporary greenness. Severe recovery classes are treated as restoration/rest priorities rather than grazing opportunities.
  6. Keep law separate from ecology. Until an authoritative closure feed is connected, the prototype never labels a site legally closed.
